The journey

How I got here.

Right now I'm working as an independent content strategist for a handful of clients. Results in a few months. I'm sharpening my own brand at the same time. Becoming Thanos, collecting all the infinity stones. Haha.

Most recent, most raw. April to June 2026.

Founding GTM at a stealth AI startup in Bengaluru. I joined because challenges excite me, and I was going to own everything. Content, product, GTM, all of it. Massive opportunity to learn.

Honest truth, I had my best two months here. But I joined too early. The product was still under development, and I couldn't create enough value for the team. That wasn't acceptable to me. If I'm a founding member and I'm not giving my best, I'm not justifying my seat. So I left.

Before that. March 2026.

Growth intern at Human Archive, YC W26, based in Goa. Honestly, I had zero idea how I was going to survive an operations role. But the environment was great, the people were great, and for the longer run I knew operations wasn't going to be my thing.

What I did get was massive. The amount I learned from my seniors, and the experience of being inside a YC accelerator, was something else. Easily one of my best internships.

Before that. October 2024 to June 2025.

Lead Generation at Vert. Remote. This one was my turning point. Vert gave me the security and the money to fck around and explore, which is what my twenties were supposed to be for. I got in through a friend's referral. Worked with the founder in the early days.

My output landed instantly. Partnerships were performing, and that visibility was the best motivation I've had at any job. The craft

How I actually work.

Not a framework from a book. Just what I run for every client, every time, with three field rules at the end that took me a while to learn.

Branding, before we write.

Voice, visual language, positioning. Your content identity comes first.

Every channel has a register.

Educational, entertaining, raw, aspirational. I find yours and write exclusively in it.

Data shapes the story.

Deep dive into what your audience actually watches, searches for, and shares.

Find the gaps, fill them.

Study what's already working in your niche. Spot what everyone is missing. Position to own that lane.

Relevant. Timely. Searchable.

Not just trending. The topics specific to your audience's intent and stage of awareness.

Hook. Build. Payoff.

Every line earns its place. Scripts built to hold from the first second to the last.

Half the battle is being found.

Optimised for search engines and AI answer engines both. Discoverability isn't optional anymore.

The script test.

I read it out loud. If I feel the vibe, it's a go. If I'm confused, I hand it to random people and watch how they react. That's the test.

The hook diagnosis.

First rule of sales, they can't feel sold. If a hook worked for a creator, don't clone it. Ask yourself, would I stop for this? Honest answer.

The long-form truth.

Perfect scripts flop. Weird ones win. The one you think is perfect won't perform. The one you think won't work will break the internet. So, be authentic, stay relevant.
